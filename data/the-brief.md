# The Bookshop Trail: a note from the committee

*What we have, what we want, and the words we use. Written for whoever is
going to build the database, by people who are not going to.*

## Who we are

We are thirty-three independent bookshops in Scotland, Wales and England,
including the three book towns of Hay-on-Wye, Wigtown and Sedbergh, and we
have joined up as the Bookshop Trail. Some of us are in the same town (there
are two in Wigtown, two in Hay, two in Edinburgh, two in Glasgow, two in York,
two in London) and some are a long way from anyone. Between the shops there
are walking routes, mostly short, one or two long, and a couple of shops in
the south west that are not really walkable from anywhere.

## What we want

A website, with a map. People should be able to find a shop by town, by
region, by country, and by what it is known for. They should be able to see
what events are coming up and who is appearing. They should be able to find a
book and see which shops have it and what it costs there. We would like the
trail itself to be walkable on the site: where you can get to on foot from a
given shop, and how far it is.

We would also like the site to be right. The guidebooks disagree about when
some of the shops opened, and we would rather the site said so than picked
one.

## What we have

- A spreadsheet of the shops: name, town, year opened, area, staff, whether
  they sell second-hand, whether there is a cafe, the website if there is
  one, and what the shop specialises in.
- A list of the towns and which council area, region and country each is in.
  England has regions; Scotland and Wales do not, or not in the same way.
- The events for the year: what kind of event, the date, which shop, which
  author, how many came, what a ticket cost. Readings, signings, launches,
  workshops, panels, book clubs, lectures.
- A list of books: title, author, publisher, year, ISBN, pages, price,
  language, genre, and the series if it is in one. Some are translations of
  others in the list. The older ones have no ISBN.
- The publishers, and which are imprints of which. Some of the imprints have
  imprints.
- Each shop's stock list: which books, how many copies, and the shelf price,
  which is not always the cover price.
- The trail segments: from which shop to which, how far, and whether the
  route is waymarked.
- The authors: where they are based, when they were born and (for some) died,
  what language they write in, and who they say influenced them.

## The words we use

We are not consistent and we know it.

- We say **book** for the title, for the edition, and for the copy on the
  shelf. "Do you have the book?" can mean any of them.
- We say **author** for anyone who wrote anything, and also for the person
  appearing at an event, who might be a translator.
- We say **shop**, **bookshop** and occasionally **store**. Some of us are
  also the building, and some of us have moved building and kept the name.
- **Area** means the floor area of a shop in square metres, and also a
  council area. Context usually makes it clear. Usually.
- **Genre**, **category**, **section** and **specialism** get used for the
  same list of things: crime, poetry, nature writing and so on. The list has
  levels: cosy crime and tartan noir are kinds of crime fiction, which is a
  kind of fiction. A shop specialises in one; a book is in one or more.
- A **publisher** might be the group or the imprint. On the book it is the
  imprint.
- **Wigtown** is the town, and it is also in the name of a festival, a
  council ward and a bay. When we say a shop is in Wigtown we mean the town.
- **Founded** and **opened** mean the same thing for a shop. Where the
  guidebooks disagree we would like both dates kept, with who said which.
- Being **on the trail** is something a shop is, for now. Shops have left,
  and one has joined twice.

## Questions we get asked, and would like the site to answer

1. Which shops are in Wales? In the south west? In a given council area?
2. Which towns on the trail have no bookshop at the moment?
3. What can I walk to from The Inkwell, and how far is it?
4. Which shops specialise in crime fiction, including the kinds of crime
   fiction?
5. Who has appeared at events in York this year, and what did it cost to go?
6. Which books have no ISBN, and why?
7. Which authors were influenced, directly or at several removes, by Rhona
   Blackwood?
8. Which imprints belong, in the end, to the Lodestone Group?
9. When did Ex Libris open, and who says so?
10. Which shops stock a given book, and what is the cheapest copy?

We can answer most of these from the spreadsheets, slowly, and we get
different answers depending on who does it. That is the problem.
